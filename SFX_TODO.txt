Search terms

Save as the filename shown, into apps/mobile/public/sounds/.

solar-lens.mp3 — 1.8s · needs a rising build to 1.15s, then ignition

▎ energy charge up and fire · sci-fi laser charge blast · plasma beam charge · riser impact · heat ray

The one clip where the tail matters as much as the attack. Look for something with an audible crescendo — "riser" is the term stock libraries use for exactly this shape.

event-horizon.mp3 — 1.5s · swelling inhale → collapse at 0.9s → bass thump

▎ implosion · black hole · reverse explosion · vacuum suck whoosh · sci-fi portal collapse · gravity well

"Reverse explosion" is the highest-yield search — a reversed boom is exactly the physics here, and plenty of libraries publish them pre-reversed.

null-pulse.mp3 — 0.9s · short, cold, no boom

▎ EMP pulse · sci-fi power down · energy drain · electromagnetic pulse · system shutdown whoosh

Wants a descending pitch, not a hit. If it has any fire or debris in it, it's wrong for this one.

mirv-salvo.mp3 — 1.5s · six thuds at 120/280/400/560/720/880ms, then one big detonation

▎ cluster bomb · carpet bombing run · artillery barrage · mortar salvo · distant multiple explosions

The hardest to match, because the rhythm is specific. A "bomb run" recording with a natural stutter gets you most of the way; don't chase exact alignment.

guillotine.mp3 — 1.1s · hard slash at t=0, then let it go quiet

▎ sword slash · katana slice · blade swoosh metal · laser cut · whip crack

Take a clean short slash and stop there. The 200ms of silence after the blade is the entire point of this effect — a clip that keeps rumbling through it destroys the beat.

kinetic-rod.mp3 — 1.4s · descending whistle → heavy crack at ~600ms

▎ incoming artillery whistle impact · meteor strike · mortar incoming · bunker buster · heavy projectile impact

railgun.mp3 — 1.65s · electrical charge → sharp discharge at 850ms

▎ railgun fire · gauss rifle shot · coil gun · capacitor discharge · electric cannon

Practical constraints

- No leading silence. playStrikeSound starts at currentTime = 0 the same instant the CSS animation begins, so any padding at the head of the file reads as lag. Trim the attack right up to the waveform.
- The first ~100ms is what sells sync. The tail matters far less — if a clip has the right attack and a wrong-ish tail, take it.
- Roughly match or undershoot the duration. Audio isn't stopped on unmount (pausing can throw AbortError), so a long tail keeps playing after the sheet closes.
- Keep them small and CC0. The existing two are 24KB and 84KB. Freesound (filter to CC0), Pixabay Sound Effects, and Mixkit are the usual sources; mono is fine.

Drop whatever you find into public/sounds/ and tell me — wiring each one is a single soundPath line in effects.ts, and the ZAP/BOOM family constants disappear as they get replaced. Anything you don't find keeps its current shared file with no work needed.
